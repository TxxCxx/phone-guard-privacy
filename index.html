English

1. The short version

  • Phone Guard does not require an account.
  • The current version does not request Android's Internet permission.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK.
  • Protection information is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer or third parties.

This policy applies to the Phone Guard / 手机防盗守护 Android app. The app uses on-device information to provide pocket protection, stationary pickup protection, device checks, alarms, notifications, and a limited local history.

2. Information handled on your device

  • Sensors: proximity and motion signals needed for the protection mode or device check you choose.
  • Device and system status: screen and lock status, availability of a secure device lock and suitable sensors, notification and background-operation status, battery restrictions, alarm and vibration availability, and rotation status.
  • Settings and protection state: language, selected protection mode, alarm settings, whether protection is armed, relevant event times, risk reason, alarm result, and limited diagnostic state.
  • Local history: up to 20 recent protection-event summaries. Phone Guard does not keep a continuous archive of raw sensor readings.
  • Owner-verification result: Android performs authentication. Phone Guard receives only success, cancellation, or failure; it does not receive or store fingerprints, facial templates, PINs, patterns, or passwords.

This information is used only to run protection you start, detect relevant events, start or stop an alarm, show status, check device compatibility, remember your settings, restore necessary runtime state, and display local history.

3. Android permissions and system access

  • Sensors: process proximity and motion signals for the selected protection mode. Phone Guard does not use a microphone, camera, or location for these features.
  • Notifications, foreground service, and wake lock: continue protection you started while the app is not visible or the screen is off, show a persistent status, and complete time-sensitive alarm processing.
  • Alarm sound and vibration: alert you when the selected protection mode detects a relevant event.
  • System authentication: confirm the device owner before ending certain protection sessions. Authentication remains under Android's control.
  • Battery settings: open Android's settings so you can choose whether to reduce operating-system interruptions. The app cannot grant itself an exception.
  • Modify system settings: only after you separately grant Android's special access, and only when you tap the in-app action that restores portrait rotation lock. Phone Guard cannot bypass Android's authorization screen.

You can review or revoke permissions in Android settings. A feature may stop working when access required for that feature is unavailable.

4. Local storage and deletion

Phone Guard stores settings, necessary runtime state, compatibility results, and event summaries in Android app-private storage. App cloud backup and device-to-device data transfer are disabled. Sensor streams are processed for the active protection session; only limited state and summaries are retained.

  • The 20 most recent protection-event summaries can be cleared from the History screen.
  • Clearing Phone Guard's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes all information stored locally by the app.

Phone Guard has no user account and the developer keeps no server copy of this app data.

5. Network, sharing, and other services

The current app cannot open its own Internet connection because it does not request Android's Internet permission. It does not transmit, sell, or share sensor signals, protection history, settings, or authentication results with the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Android, Google Play, and your device manufacturer may independently process distribution, installation, backup, security, or system-diagnostic information under their own policies. Phone Guard does not receive that platform data.

This privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical web request information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page stores only your English/Chinese choice in your browser; Phone Guard does not receive GitHub's access logs or the saved language choice.

6. Security, changes, and contact

Phone Guard reduces exposure through on-device processing, Android app-private storage, disabled backups, limited history, and no app Internet permission. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. Phone Guard is published by TxxCxx. Privacy questions may be sent to hf19880808@gmail.com.

中文

1. 简要说明

  • 手机防盗守护不要求注册账号。
  • 当前版本不申请 Android 互联网权限。
  • 应用不包含广告、分析、崩溃上报或跟踪 SDK。
  • 守护信息只在您的设备上处理，不会发送给开发者或第三方。

本政策适用于“手机防盗守护 / Phone Guard”Android 应用。应用在本机处理必要信息， 用于提供离袋守护、静置防拿、设备检查、报警、通知和有限的本机历史记录。

2. 在设备上处理的信息

  • 传感器：用户所选守护模式或设备检查需要的接近与运动信号。
  • 设备与系统状态：屏幕与锁定状态、安全锁屏和所需传感器是否可用、通知与后台运行状态、 电池限制、报警与振动能力以及屏幕旋转状态。
  • 设置与守护状态：语言、守护模式、报警设置、守护是否武装、相关事件时间、风险原因、 报警结果和有限的诊断状态。
  • 本机历史：最多保存最近 20 条守护事件摘要。应用不会持续保存原始传感器数据档案。
  • 主人验证结果：身份验证由 Android 系统完成。应用只接收成功、取消或失败结果， 不会获得或保存指纹、面部模板、PIN、图案或密码。

上述信息只用于运行您主动开启的守护、识别相关事件、启动或停止报警、显示状态、检查设备兼容性、 记住设置、恢复必要运行状态以及显示本机历史。

3. Android 权限与系统访问

  • 设备传感器：为所选守护模式处理接近与运动信号。这些功能不使用麦克风、摄像头或定位。
  • 通知、前台服务与唤醒锁：在应用不可见或屏幕关闭时继续运行您已开启的守护， 显示持续状态，并完成有时效要求的报警处理。
  • 报警音与振动：在所选守护模式检测到相关事件时提醒您。
  • 系统身份验证：在结束部分守护会话前确认设备主人；验证过程始终由 Android 管理。
  • 电池设置：打开 Android 系统页面，由您决定是否减少系统对守护运行的中断； 应用不能自行获得电池优化例外。
  • 修改系统设置：仅在您另行授予 Android 特殊访问权限后，并且仅在您点击应用内 “一键恢复竖屏锁定”时修改旋转设置；应用无法绕过 Android 授权页面。

您可以在 Android 设置中查看或撤销权限；缺少某项必要权限时，对应功能可能无法使用。

4. 本地保存与删除

应用设置、必要运行状态、设备兼容性结果和事件摘要保存在 Android 应用专用存储中。 应用数据的云备份和设备间迁移已关闭。传感器数据流只用于当前守护会话；应用仅保留有限状态和事件摘要。

  • 最近 20 条守护事件摘要可以在应用的“历史记录”页面清除。
  • 在 Android 设置中清除应用存储或卸载应用，会删除应用保存在本机的全部信息。

应用没有用户账户，开发者服务器上也没有这些应用数据的副本。

5. 网络、共享与其他服务

当前应用没有申请 Android 互联网权限，因此不能自行建立网络连接。应用不会向开发者、广告商、 分析服务商或数据经纪商传输、出售或共享传感器信号、守护历史、应用设置或身份验证结果。

Android、Google Play 和设备厂商可能依据各自政策，独立处理应用分发、安装、备份、安全或系统诊断信息； 手机防盗守护不会收到这些平台数据。

本隐私政策网页由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 可能依据 GitHub 一般隐私声明 处理网页请求技术信息。本页面只在浏览器中保存中英文选择；手机防盗守护不会收到 GitHub 的访问日志或该语言选择。

6. 安全、政策更新与联系

应用通过本机处理、Android 应用专用存储、关闭备份、限制历史条数和不申请互联网权限来减少数据暴露。 任何安全措施都无法保证绝对安全。

重大变化会在本页面发布并更新生效日期。“手机防盗守护”由 TxxCxx 发布。 如有隐私问题，请联系 hf19880808@gmail.com。
